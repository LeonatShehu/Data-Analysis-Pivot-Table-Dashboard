## Project insight
Prishtina’s real estate market in 2025 shows a clear premium zone effect, 
with central and well-established neighborhoods commanding the highest prices, while some districts are more price-sensitive and fluctuate more.

## Final conclusion:
Bregu i Diellit had the highest average transaction price overall, around €168k.
Dardania and Velania were also premium areas, both near €160k+ on average.
Kalabria and Qendra showed strong pricing too, with Qendra having the highest price per m².
Arbëria, Ulpiana, and Mati 1 were generally more affordable, with lower average total prices.

Prices were volatile month to month rather than steadily rising.
Several neighborhoods peaked in mid-to-late year:
Bregu i Diellit rose sharply in September and November
Dardania stayed strong through summer
Kalabria and Qendra had some big spikes, especially in July, September, and December
